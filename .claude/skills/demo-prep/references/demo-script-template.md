Use this template to write the full demo script. Every presenter should rehearse from this script — not from memory. Write it in the exact words you will say, not as bullet points.

## 1. Opening Hook (30 seconds)
Start with the problem — not with your name or the product name. Make the judge feel the pain first.

Script:
> "[Problem scenario in one vivid sentence]. Every [target user] deals with this [daily/weekly]. Today we built a solution."

Example:
> "Every procurement manager in a mid-size company spends 3 hours a week chasing approval status in email threads. Today we built the tool that ends that."

---

## 2. Solution Statement (15 seconds)
One sentence. What does the product do, for whom, and what is the core value?

Script:
> "[Product name] is a [what it is] that lets [who] [do what], so that [benefit]."

Example:
> "RequestFlow is a procurement request platform that lets employees submit and track approvals in real time, so managers spend less time chasing and more time deciding."

---

## 3. Live Demo Sequence (2 minutes)

Write each step as a specific action. Include what you will say while performing it.

**Rule:** Every step must be completable in under 30 seconds. Rehearse with a timer.

| Step | Action on screen | Words to say |
|------|-----------------|-------------|
| 1 | Navigate to [URL/page] | "Here's the dashboard — you can see [X]." |
| 2 | Click [button name] | "Let me create a new request." |
| 3 | Fill in [form fields] | "I'm submitting a request for [example data]." |
| 4 | Submit | "One click to submit." |
| 5 | Show result | "It's immediately in the list, flagged as Pending." |
| 6 | Show [AI or key feature] | "Here's where the AI [does X] — notice [benefit]." |
| 7 | Show [second action] | "The approver sees this in their queue instantly." |

**Do not ad-lib during the demo sequence.** If something unexpected happens, follow the fallback plan.

---

## 4. Value Close (30 seconds)
Bring it back to business impact. Use numbers where credible.

Script:
> "What you just saw eliminates [X hours / Y manual steps / Z days of delay]. For a team of [size], that's [impact]. The whole thing is built on [key technical choice], and it works end-to-end right now."

---

## 5. Roadmap Tease (15 seconds)
Name 1–2 cut features to show vision. Do not apologize for not building them.

Script:
> "We have the core flow working today. Next we'd add [P2 feature 1] and [P2 feature 2] — both are designed and ready to build."
